Beneath Quiet Streets

This text appears on a black screen on game start: Detective, I know it’s out of the ordinary, but the small town of Cedar Valley needs your expertise. Their Sheriff’s office has been overwhelmed by a string of missing persons and possible homicides. Most recently, a real estate acquisitions man named Max Alden was found dead in his penthouse. Apparently neighbors later reported hearing five gunshots the night before, but their guys haven’t found any bullet holes, and the death seemed to be due to blunt trauma to the head on their initial inspection. This is only the latest in an ever growing string of missing persons and unexplained deaths, which may or may not be related. You are to start by meeting the Sheriff at the Diner at the edge of town. Conduct a thorough investigation into Max Alden’s death and any other suspicious activities related to these cases, and report back with your findings. Good luck. The game starts. You are a detective, standing near a car on the outskirts of town, having just driven in. The small town is in a valley surrounded by tall mountains. A large wheat field is behind you along the winding two lane road. The diner is ahead. On a streetlight nearby is posted: Missing Poster
You approach the diner. A song plays faintly from inside, swelling as you enter. A jukebox is in the corner, playing the song. You walk up to the counter. There's a freshly poured cup of coffee on the counter, but you don't see anyone else in the diner. You ring the bell on the counter a few times. No answer. Suddenly, the music from the jukebox cuts, and morse code begins playing out of the jukebox. Glancing around the diner, you see a morse code decoder paper on a chair.
