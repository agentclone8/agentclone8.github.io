Time Clash: Year One

A choice-based interactive story combined with a time-management simulator. Game design document, 21 pages.

The player gets to create a character and live the life of a high school student training to be a peacekeeper in a world where medieval times, modern day, and the future coexist. The player’s choices will have consequences throughout the story – both explicit choices and their gameplay style affect the story’s outcome.

  • Your Choices Matter. Major choices, Dialogue choices, and even Gameplay choices all factor into your story and other characters’ perceptions of you.
  • Every Moment is a Choice. Stories will often progress with or without you. So when you have to choose between helping your friends save the school or keeping your date with your crush, choose wisely.
  • Theme. The theme of the game is that what we do and how we spend time matters. Even the decision to not act is a decision that will have consequences. What do we really value in life? Our friends? Romantic partners? Helping strangers? Our own money or power? Or doing the right thing?
  • Background. The player will choose whether they come from a poor, rich, or middle-class background. This will determine which students will accept the player right off the bat, and which will be prejudiced against the player.
  • Structure. This game will use a branch and bottleneck structure, but this will be prevented from destroying lasting consequences through statistics and variables which will come back later in the story.
